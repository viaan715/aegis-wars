CONTRACT FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
This Agreement is entered into on March 4, 2021, between Alpha Logistics Inc. and Beta Consulting LLC.
The total contract value is $120,000, payable in four installments.
Beta Consulting LLC shall deliver the final report no later than October 15, 2021.
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
