The agreement was signed on March 4, 2021 between Alpha Logistics Inc. and Beta Consulting LLC.

The total contract value is $120,000, paid in four installments.

Beta Consulting LLC must deliver the final report by December 1, 2021.

Disputes will be resolved through binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

The agreement includes a non-compete clause preventing Beta Consulting from working with competitors for five years.
